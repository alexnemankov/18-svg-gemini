BOTANICAL NOTES / 04

Observation of Bloom Morphology

Sculpted petals enveloping the core exhibit individual fullness, bilateral asymmetry, and torsion. Unfurling from the receptacle, surface relaxation, and bilateral edge curling are continuously modulated in a single blooming action. Numerical formulas are artistic botanical approximations rather than physical stress simulations.

  1. Petal Surface Expansion
    Cell expansion drives petal growth during anthesis. Formed as circular sheets, petals combine non-uniform expansion with unfolding geometry (Yamada et al., 2009).
  2. Bud Curvature & Belly Bulge
    Rounded petals envelope the floral core, bulging outward at the bud's midpoint. Outer petals begin opening first, followed by middle whorls, while central nested petals stay low until late anthesis (Han et al., 2019).
  3. Nested Helical Overlap
    Inner petals follow concentric logarithmic spirals with angular leads, creating the classic vortex formation when viewed from above (+Y).
  4. Coupled Margin Roll
    As petals unfurl, bilateral conical edge rolls curl outward, meeting at the distal tip cusp (Zhang et al., Science, 2025).
  5. Basal Growth & Asymmetric Flare
    Asymmetric basal expansion continuously shifts the meridian curvature from base to tip (Cheng et al., 2021).
  6. Stem, Leaflet & Sepal Architecture
    Alternate pinnately compound leaves (3 to 5 leaflets) with serrated margins, stipules, prickles, and 5 glandular sepals (NC State Extension / Rosa).
